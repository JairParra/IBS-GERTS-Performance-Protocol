      Section 02

      Nutritionist Advice on Specifics

      Ingredients asked about specifically — the professional verdict on each during this protocol.

      Legumes

      Prohibited

      While legumes may feel like they aid digestion, they are explicitly classified under the Absolute Nutritional No's. They contain highly fermentable oligosaccharides that produce gas in the intestines (triggering IBS), which pushes upward against the stomach — increasing sphincter pressure and triggering acid reflux.

      Do not integrate legumes during this healing phase.

      Berries

      Approved · Limited

      To respect both IBS (FODMAP) and reflux (acidity) constraints, blueberries are the safest option — non-acidic and low-fermentable.

      Blueberries only — limit to ½ cup per serving.

      Soy Sauce & Noodles

      Approved · Moderation

      Light soy sauce is approved in moderation to achieve Asian flavor profiles, provided it has no hidden garlic or onion extracts. White rice vermicelli is highly recommended as a safe, easily digestible carbohydrate.

      1 cup cooked white vermicelli = 3 S.T.E.P.S.
      Section 03

      Customized Portion Architecture

      Structured for a 65 kg highly active male — supporting baseline metabolism plus four intense weekly training sessions, without overloading the stomach or relaxing the LES.

      Protein

      Muscle Maintenance
      130–140 g / day
      Anchor of every meal
      • Meals: 120 g of chicken/poultry, meat, or fish per meal.
      • Daily supplement: 25 g wheat protein + 5 g creatine in skim lactose-free or almond milk, as one daily snack.
      • Eggs: at least 2 large eggs daily (≈ 12 g protein).

      Carbohydrates · S.T.E.P.S.

      Training Fuel
      2–3 S.T.E.P.S. / meal
      1 S.T.E.P.S. = 15 g net carbs → 30–45 g per meal
      • Because of the high activity level, 1 S.T.E.P.S. per meal is too low for this profile.
      • Reference: 1 medium Russell potato = 2 S.T.E.P.S. · 1 cup cooked white vermicelli = 3 S.T.E.P.S.
      • Double training days (Monday): bump to 3–4 S.T.E.P.S. around the workout windows.

      Fats

      LES-Safe Dosing
      4–5 small servings / day
      Spread evenly across meals & snacks
      • Heavy, fatty meals delay LES closure — calories cannot come from large fat inputs.
      • Per meal: e.g. 5 ml garlic-infused oil.
      • Per snack: e.g. 15 ml walnuts.
